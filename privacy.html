Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

1. About this application

Personal Finance is a private, non-commercial application developed for personal financial management by its owner.

2. Data processed

The application may access account information, balances and transaction data from financial institutions explicitly authorised by the account owner through Enable Banking.

3. Purpose and use

Data is used exclusively for personal financial tracking, expense categorisation, reconciliation and reporting.

4. Data storage and security

Financial data is stored locally on the owner's devices. API credentials and private cryptographic keys are kept confidential and are not intentionally shared with third parties.

5. Data sharing

Personal financial data is not sold or shared for advertising or commercial purposes.

6. Data retention and deletion

The owner controls the retention and deletion of locally stored financial information.
